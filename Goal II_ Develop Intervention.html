Goal II: Develop Intervention

Human Resources
Product: LEAD 411 Selection and Training Process.pptx

I designed a selection and training process based on a job analysis and personal interview of a Director of Human Resources, researching knowledge, skills, and abilities (KSAs), and best practices in HR selection and training. Data collection methods for my research included a personal interview, supporting documents shared by my interviewee – Behavioral Interview Questions, Job Description Template – and LinkedIn job search. The Power Point presentation highlighted methods to identify experienced applicants by using the STAR method - Situation, Task, Action, Result. This learning experience demonstrated the Workplace Professional Capabilities – career and leadership development, organizational development and culture, talent strategy management, and performance improvement.

Adult Learning and Diversity
Product: LEAD 460 Bridging Generational Differences.docx

The research assignment examines the generational differences in the workplace and refining organizational practices, training, and research that challenges how generation is defined while encompassing the related impact theories of generation (age, cohort, and period). Shifting organizational thinking from categorical generational differences to lifespan dynamics and shared consciousness. The lifespan development framework considers historical and sociocultural influences and how organizations can support life-long learning for their employees, bringing the work-life experience into balance, increasing satisfaction and productivity, and impacting organizational change in adult learning, diversity, and inclusion.

Basic Skills for Student Success
Product: LEAD 498 Strengths Reflection.docx

The three guiding constructs for this reflection are inquiry, action, and advocacy. The focus in this reflection is a career and personal goal setting by identifying my dimensions of wellness and personal strengths, composing a strategic, wellness-oriented plan, collecting information from multiple sources and integrating my lived experiences, all in progress toward my goals. The key takeaway is being purposeful and deliberate in setting goals through techniques such as Mind Mapping & Brainstorming, Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs - Reflection Practice and character strengths assessment, to be powerful, successful, and lead organizational change and success for myself and others.
