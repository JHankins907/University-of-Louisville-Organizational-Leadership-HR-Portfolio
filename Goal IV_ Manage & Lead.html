Goal IV: Manage & Lead

Supporting Organizational Change
Product: LEAD 442 Change Research Report.docx
This research report examines the acquisition of engineering and architecture firms by a major corporation. The aim of my research examined how the acquired managers’ experience makes sense of the changes, how those frames contrast with the acquiring managers’ experience, and how the relationship between the two influences that adjustment. Experiences with acquiring members frame the sensemaking of the newcomers and adjustment to the new role frame – perceived and actual – construct the meanings of relationships to the new firm and its acquiring managers. I also provide insights and strategies, based on professional experience and knowledge gleaned from my research. Strategies for successful acquisition integration that utilize intervention methods to integrate individual, teams, departments, and organization – focusing on onboarding in-person sessions and orientations, team-start up and manager development, strategic planning and goal-setting, leadership development to align missions and goals, and ultimately organizational design that removes gridlock and promotes growth, emphasizing that vital behaviors must connect to intrinsic satisfaction and social support to effect change.

Workplace and Information Ethics 
Product: LEAD 587 Cyber Bullying.pptx

I designed a Power Point presentation on Remote Workplace Cyber Bullying. Key objectives: 1) define workplace cyber bullying and its effects, 2) identify acts of cyber bullying, and 3) how to safeguard against cyber bullying. Remote work and virtual interactions increased during the COVID pandemic and so did cyber bullying. Cyber bullying crosses industries and has effects not only on organizations, but its employees with phycological implications, impacting careers, mental stability, and carrying over to families. The impact highlights the importance of organizations developing policies and guidelines to safeguard employees and set policies that address violations.
